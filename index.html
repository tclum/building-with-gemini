Policies

Late Assignment Policy

Each online week starts on Monday 12:01AM and ends Sunday 10PM. Assignments must be turned in on time for full credit. No late assignments are accepted unless the instructor is notified at least 24 hours in advance and given a valid reason. This is a firm policy and the only exceptions will be for very extreme situations such as documented medical emergencies (being sick is not a medical emergency).

Attendance Policy

This is an in-person. Attendance is expected because students will participate in group discussions and case studies, and ultimately be graded for participation. If a student expects to miss a class, they must notify the instructor at least 24 hours in advance and give a valid reason in order to make up the assignment.

Use of Artificial Intelligence (AI)

AI is permitted in this class. It is allowed with attribution, meaning that a student must credit the source of the AI information. To protect the privacy of all students and maintain a safe learning environment, students are not permitted to use AI-based tools (e.g., Otter.ai, Fireflies.ai, Zoom AI Companion) to record, transcribe, or analyze class conversations.
